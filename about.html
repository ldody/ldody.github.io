About

I am currently conducting my doctoral research at Research Center Magellan in Lyon, with international research experience at Keio University in Japan. Beyond research, I enjoy building end-to-end ML systems, exploring new quantitative approaches, and working on problems where data, modelling and decision-making meet.

My path into quantitative research has been deliberately multidisciplinary. I was originally trained in mechanical engineering, with a specialization in fluid mechanics and energetics, before moving toward business management, artificial intelligence and finance. This background shaped the way I approach problems: combining mathematical modelling, engineering practice and an understanding of real-world constraints.

I am a quantitative researcher and machine learning engineer with a PhD background in AI and financial markets. My work sits at the intersection of applied machine learning, quantitative modelling and financial data, with a focus on building methods that can move from research questions to working systems.
