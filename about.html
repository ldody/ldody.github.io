About

Quantitative researcher and PhD candidate in AI and Financial Markets, working at the intersection of financial machine learning, market microstructure, deep learning and reinforcement learning.

My research focuses on high-frequency financial data, market forecasting, liquidity modelling and portfolio decision-making. I develop end-to-end quantitative research pipelines, from data processing and feature engineering to model training, validation and backtesting. My research experience spans France and Japan, including a visiting research fellowship at Keio University focused on AI, reinforcement learning and portfolio optimization.

Originally trained as a mechanical engineer, with a specialization in fluid mechanics and energetics, I progressively moved toward artificial intelligence and quantitative finance. I also hold a degree in business management, giving me a multidisciplinary background spanning engineering, AI, finance and management.

Alongside my research, I have taught financial market microstructure at university level to both Bachelor's and Master's students.
